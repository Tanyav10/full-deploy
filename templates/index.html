Meet the Founder

Guided by Supriya Verma

Founder & Lead Instructor

Supriya Verma

Supriya founded Suyogya Yoga Centre with a simple belief — that yoga should be accessible, correctly taught, and genuinely restorative, not performative. Her classes blend classical Hatha yoga with breathwork and mindful alignment, shaped around each student's body and pace.

"Yoga isn't about touching your toes. It's about what you learn on the way down."

Under her guidance, Suyogya has grown into a warm, disciplined space for students of every age — from first-timers to expecting mothers to seniors seeking gentle, sustainable movement.

Our Story

A quiet, disciplined space to come back to your breath.

Suyogya Yoga Centre began as one teacher's commitment to correct, compassionate yoga instruction — and has grown into a full community practice space in Aliganj, Lucknow.

Founder & Lead Instructor

Supriya Verma

Supriya Verma founded Suyogya Yoga Centre to bring an authentic, well-taught yoga practice to the Aliganj community — one built on proper alignment, patient instruction and genuine care for every student who walks in.

Her teaching draws on classical Hatha yoga, pranayama and meditation, adapted thoughtfully for beginners, working professionals, seniors, and — most recently — expecting mothers through the centre's new Pregnancy Yoga programme.
